## Production workflow

1. Generate related deliverables in batches while keeping each ID/file independent.
2. Run one QC pass over the batch, then revise any failures.
3. Keep drafts marked `queued` until a real file exists; after creation, record source, checks, and status here.
4. Legal and tax templates are operational starting points only; do not represent them as enforceable or jurisdiction-compliant without professional review.
