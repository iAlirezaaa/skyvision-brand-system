# BG-01 — Primary Dark Hero Background

**Status:** draft visual created in conversation, not uploaded to the public GitHub repository; not approved.  
**Priority:** P0  
**Output:** one standalone landscape image at `assets/production/BG/BG-01.png`  
**Working target:** 16:9; ideally 3840 × 2160 px if genuinely exported at that resolution.

## Objective
Create the defining background for Sky Vision's brand covers and website hero: evocative without competing with navigation, headlines or task content.

## Non-negotiable reference language
- Navy negative space, warm ivory and muted powder/mist blue wave ribbons.
- Fine gold orbit arcs, elegant celestial marks and sparse compass stars.
- Handcrafted, painterly **2D** graphic look; soft irregular cloud shapes.
- Center composition anchored by a large orbital ring or spacious halo; small, supporting stars.
- No fake metallic 3D embossing, photoreal textures, neon, excessive noise or unrelated characters.

## Composition
- 16:9 landscape; dominant navy field, one central refined orbital motif and gentle flowing accents at edges.
- Keep a clean text-safe region. No words, logos or UI elements burned into the art.
- Use one coherent art direction. Avoid making a brand collage.
