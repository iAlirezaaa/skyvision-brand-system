## Objective

Create the defining dark background for Sky Vision brand covers and the website hero: evocative without competing with headlines, navigation or product content.

## Non-negotiable reference language

- Midnight navy negative space, warm ivory and desaturated mist-blue wave ribbons.
- Fine gold orbit arcs, elegant celestial marks and sparse compass stars.
- Handcrafted, painterly **2D** graphic look; soft irregular cloud shapes.
- An open circular orbital motif or spacious halo with small supporting stars.
- No fake metallic 3D embossing, photoreal textures, neon, excessive noise or unrelated characters.

## Composition

- Landscape 16:9 with a dominant navy field and one coherent central/near-central orbital motif.
- Preserve a clean text-safe region. No words, logos or UI burned into the artwork.
- Deliver one image, not a brand board or collage.
