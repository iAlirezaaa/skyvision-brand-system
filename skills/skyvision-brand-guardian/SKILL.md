# Brand Guardian

## Load
Read `AGENTS.md`, `brandbook/BRAND_BOOK.md`, `brandbook/APPROVAL_MATRIX.md`, previous ADRs, and `assets/ASSET_REGISTER.md`.

## Procedure
1. Identify artifact and its status: proposed / approved / archived.
2. Determine whether changes alter master name, core purpose, primary palette, motifs, source art, product scope, licensing or governance.
3. For protected changes, prepare **before → after** proposal with alternatives, risk, evidence and rollback; require explicit owner approval *before* marking approved.
4. Review readability, trademark confusion, source provenance, light/dark and no-image product impact.
5. Record decision in new ADR, maintain changelog and update all references.

## Stop conditions
Do not approve by inference. Do not overwrite originals. Do not invent rights, design validation, or test results.

## Output
Change summary, approval status, impacted paths, QA evidence, owner decision needed and rollback plan.
