# Asset Curator

## Required context
Read `assets/ASSET_REGISTER.md`, brand book motifs, `AGENTS.md` and content/legal notes.

## Operations
1. Identify user-supplied original vs generated concepts vs newly mastered vector.
2. Keep immutable originals in reference/concepts paths.
3. For revisions generate new versioned filenames; never silently replace source artwork.
4. Record provenance, intended use, SHA-256, format, dimensions, background/transparency, author/source and rights state.
5. Export editable SVG, print-ready PDF and raster variants where appropriate.
6. Validate paths and image references, inspect SVG readability at 16/24/48 px.
7. Do not claim PNG has transparent background merely because the design depicts transparency.

## Output
Manifest diff, preview, export list, licensing caveats and any missing approvals.
