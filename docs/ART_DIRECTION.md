# Art Direction and Motif Grammar

**Status:** Proposed / not final brand sign-off.

## Visual anchor
Deep navy negative space; warm cream/ivory shapes; desaturated blue flowing ribbons; sparse sand-gold orbital geometry; clouds and stars as supporting forms. The look is poetic 2D illustrative, not photoreal 3D.

## Construction constraints
- **Orbit:** thin arcs, optional one central axis, no cluttered technical diagram noise.
- **Wave:** 2–4 overlapping flowing ribbons; gentle separation between cream and blue.
- **Cloud:** stylized curved clusters with calm, restrained edges.
- **Star:** sparse cross/compass symbol; small at product size.
- **Moon:** one signature crescent, not repeated on every surface.
- **Space:** let central void breathe; no ornamental full-frame border around task tables.

## Functional hierarchy
1. Essential information.
2. Spacing and typography.
3. State/selection.
4. Optional decoration.

## Do
Use navy and ivory for identity, small gold details for focus, and well-separated wave shapes in marketing. Use stripped-back icons in task grids.

## Don't
No heavy gradients or luminous glow on dashboards, illegible gold text, textured backgrounds behind forms, repeated constellation rings in rows, or decorative UI pretending to be a functional control.

## Theme notes
Dark and light are one system with two expressions. Use semantic tokens and measure contrast, not approximate it by eye.

## Deliverables
Primary/compact/mono symbols, repeatable patterns, web and print exports, color proofs, and separate art direction samples for brand storytelling and product UI.
